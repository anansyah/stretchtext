StretchText

Click a phrase and it grows exactly there. Click the grown passage and it folds back.

This demo calls gen.pollinations.ai directly with your key. Nothing is stored on a server.

Stretchtext is a hypertext feature that lets a reader expand a passage in place, without leaving the page or following a link. Ted Nelson described the idea in 1967 and named it in 1970, with two throttles: one for navigation and one for expansion. Writing every level by hand was too much work, so it never caught on outside hypertext research and electronic literature.

Generated text makes the extra levels cheap enough to be worth writing. Turn on stretching above, then click a phrase in this paragraph and watch it grow where it stands. Each click is one chat completion, billed to your own Pollen at the model's listed rate, and the answer is cached for six hours so a second click is free.

The extension wraps exactly this: content.js decorates paragraphs, lib/api.js calls https://gen.pollinations.ai/v1/chat/completions with your key, and lib/stretch.js builds the prompt. Source · Install · pollinations.ai
